How It Works

Your Journey With Us

From first enquiry to final acquisition, we guide you through a seamless, transparent process.

Step 01

Consultation

Reach out by phone, email, or visit our showroom. Share your collecting interests, budget, and goals — we'll tailor our recommendations to your unique profile.

Step 02

Authentication

Every stamp undergoes rigorous authentication using watermark analysis, perforation measurement, UV light inspection, and comparison with reference specimens from our extensive library.

Step 03

Valuation

Our experts assess current market value using recent auction data, catalogue prices, and rarity indices. You receive a detailed report with photographs and condition notes.

Step 04

Acquisition

Once you've made your selection, we prepare museum-grade packaging, arrange insured shipping, and provide all certificates of authenticity and provenance documentation.
