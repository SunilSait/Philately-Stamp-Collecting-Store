Our Founding Story

From a Covent Garden Vault to the Foremost Curatorial Treasury

In the autumn of 1987, Senior Royal Philatelic Society Fellow Lord Arthur Vance-Sinclair established PhilaVault within a fortified subterranean cellar on London's King Street. Convinced that the classical stamp trade had grown vulnerable to unrecorded restorations, concealed paper thinnings, and bleached cancellation ink, he assembled an uncompromising curatorial team committed to scientific verification.

"A rare stamp is never merely a fragment of printed paper. It is an empire's diplomatic envoy, an epoch's masterpiece of miniature steel-die engraving, and an unforgeable witness to human civilization."
Lord Arthur Vance-Sinclair, FRPSL — Founding Curatorial Director

Institutional Timeline

Four Decades of Philatelic Milestones

Key moments that shaped our journey from a boutique London salon to the world's most trusted philatelic treasury.

1987

Founding in Covent Garden

Lord Arthur Vance-Sinclair opens the inaugural PhilaVault salon at 48 King Street, London, dedicated exclusively to Pre-1900 Great Britain and Imperial Commonwealth rarities.

1995

The Great Britain Plate Flaw Atlas

PhilaVault researchers complete a landmark 400-page curatorial census of 1,200 Penny Black and Penny Red line-engraved plates, establishing reference standards cited globally by auctioneers.

2004

First Hyperspectral Paper Laboratory

Commissioned Europe's first non-destructive hyperspectral scanner dedicated to antique paper fibers and fugitive cancellation inks, eliminating invasive chemical testing.

2012

National Museum Loan Endowment

Partnered with the British Library and Postal Heritage Trust to establish the Permanent Loan Fund, keeping 45+ historically seminal postal covers on public educational display.

2018

Geneva & Tokyo Viewing Salons

Expanded private treaty operations with sovereign consultation suites in Geneva and Tokyo, enabling secure biometric hand-courier transfers for international family offices.

2023 – Present

Cryptographic Provenance Registry

Pioneered physical-to-digital cryptographic passports for rare stamps, pairing tamper-evident Melinex slabs with verifiable cryptographic ledger records for banks and underwriters.
