Scientific Provenance

The Forensic Philatelic Laboratory

Beyond human sight: every high-value specimen undergoes non-destructive multi-spectral testing, fiber interferometry, and archival certification.

Stage 01

Multi-Spectral Luminescence

Dual-wavelength UV (365nm) and infrared examination reveals invisible repairs, bleached cancellation ink, regumming, and modern fluorescent phosphor paper.

Detection Resolution: 365nm / 254nm Dual Optical
Stage 02

Beta-Radiography & Watermarks

Non-contact beta-transmission imaging maps paper fiber density and watermark laid-lines to within 2 microns without wet chemical immersion, protecting delicate gum.

Paper Density Precision: ±2 Micron Fiber Mapping
Stage 03

Perforation Pitch Interferometry

Computerized microscopic gauging of perforation tooth cadence, gauge geometry, and die strike pressure definitively differentiates original perforations.

Gauge Calibration: ±0.02mm Optical Interferometry
Stage 04

Micro-Atmospheric Encapsulation

Archival museum slabs sonically sealed with inert nitrogen and argon. Maintains constant 45% ±2% relative humidity with 99.8% UV-absorbing optical acrylic shielding.

Archival Integrity: Zero-Acid Hermetic Enclosure
Accredited Curatorial Standards Testing protocols aligned with Royal Philatelic Society London (RPSL), American Philatelic Society (APS), and British Philatelic Association (BPA).
