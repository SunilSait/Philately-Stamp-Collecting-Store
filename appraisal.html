Step by Step

Our Appraisal Process

A transparent, rigorous process designed to give you confidence in every valuation.

1

Submission

Send us photos and details of your stamps via our online form, email, or in person at our showroom.

2

Examination

Our experts inspect each stamp under controlled conditions using specialised instruments and reference materials.

3

Grading

Each stamp receives a grade from Superb to Good based on centering, gum, cancellation, and any defects.

4

Report

You receive a detailed report with photographs, grades, market valuations, and a Certificate of Appraisal.

Industry Standards

Our Grading System

We use the internationally recognised philatelic grading scale to ensure consistency and transparency in every appraisal.

S Superb — Perfect centering, fresh colours, full original gum, no defects
XF Extremely Fine — Near-perfect centering, vibrant colours, minimal handling
VF Very Fine — Well-centered, good colour, slight gum disturbance acceptable
F Fine — Average centering, acceptable for most collections
G Good — Off-center or with minor faults, space-filler quality
