What We Purchase

Items We're Looking For

We buy a wide range of philatelic material. If it's related to postal history, we want to hear about it.

Rare Singles

High-value individual stamps from any country or period. Errors, imperforates, and inverts.

Complete Collections

Country, thematic, or period-specific collections of any size. Albums and stockbooks welcome.

First Day Covers

Commemorative envelopes with stamps cancelled on their date of issue, particularly pre-1960 examples.

Postal History

Pre-stamp covers, maritime mail, military postal history, and historically significant correspondence.

Error Stamps

Printing errors, colour shifts, missing perforations, and inverted designs — some of philately's most valuable pieces.

Mint Sheets

Full sheets and blocks in mint condition, particularly early issues and commemoratives with plate markings.
